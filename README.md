Smile Health — Homepage Redesign & Overview

1. Website Overview

Business Purpose

Smile Health GmbH operates an international patient coordination model providing specialist dental implant treatments in Germany (primarily at their main centre in Leipzig, with additional appointments in Berlin). The website aims to convert prospective international patients by establishing trust in German clinical expertise, providing transparent pricing, and outlining full-service travel coordination.

Target Audience

Primary: UK adults seeking dental implants, full-jaw restorations, and fixed zirconia bridgework.

Secondary: Patients comparing cross-border treatment costs, exploring specialized options (All-on-4, All-on-6, All-on-8), and seeking reassurance regarding travel, clinical credentials, and post-operative aftercare.

Redesign Direction

The new homepage design positions Smile Health around senior-led clinical excellence (under Dr. med. dent. Rasmus Sperber), transparent fixed-price treatment packages, and a seamless international patient journey. The overall direction focuses on reducing patient anxiety regarding cross-border dental care while driving qualified consultation enquiries.

2. Main Improvements

Fix 1: Transparent Pricing & Package Breakdown

Previous Issue: Unclear costs caused concern over hidden fees and whether quoted prices covered comprehensive treatment steps.

Implementation: Integrated clear, published pricing examples alongside specific inclusions (such as fixed-price Smile Health MasterClass™ options, recognised implant manufacturers, temporary bridgework, and 4-star hotel accommodations).

Impact: Directly addresses patient cost uncertainty and establishes immediate commercial transparency.

Fix 2: Streamlined International Journey & Logistics

Previous Issue: Lack of clarity regarding travel requirements, location splitting (Leipzig vs. Berlin), and post-treatment care created hesitation around travelling abroad for complex procedures.

Implementation: Explicitly detailed the end-to-end patient journey on the homepage, highlighting Leipzig as the primary implantology centre, Berlin airport transfers, accommodation planning, and the 5-year Smile Health guarantee.

Impact: Alleviates concerns about travel complexity, trip frequency, and ongoing communication during the healing period.

Fix 3: Direct Clinical Trust & Contact Pathways

Previous Issue: Insufficient upfront clinical verification and fragmented enquiry channels made it difficult for patients to evaluate clinical oversight before committing.

Implementation: Prominently featured clinical leadership (Dr. med. dent. Rasmus Sperber, M.Sc., M.Sc., MBA), professional regulatory credentials (Saxony Chamber of Dentists), and direct communication options including telephone (07771 970 191), official email, and dedicated WhatsApp support.

Impact: Builds immediate confidence in medical supervision and provides low-friction channels to request initial consultations.

3. Design Improvements

Visual Presentation & Trust: Incorporated professional clinical branding, clear regulatory body references, and structured badges highlighting the 5-year guarantee to reinforce credibility.

Layout & Content Hierarchy: Structured content sections systematically: starting with the core value proposition, followed by treatment options (All-on-4/6/8 vs. single implants), pricing transparency, travel logistics, and contact points.

User Experience (UX): Simplified navigation to reduce friction for prospective UK patients, guiding users naturally from information gathering to booking an initial consultation.

Consistency: Standardized contact information, company details (Leipzig address: Brühl 54, 04109 Leipzig), and official branding elements across all viewports for a cohesive experience.
