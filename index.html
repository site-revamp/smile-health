Dental implant treatments to discuss with your clinical team.

Every treatment plan should be based on an individual clinical assessment. Explore the main treatment categories and speak with the team about which options may be relevant to you.

01

All-on-4 Implants

A full-arch restoration approach using four implants where clinically suitable. Your dentist will assess your needs and explain the treatment plan.

All-on-6 Implants

A journey you can understand

From your first enquiry to treatment and aftercare.

International treatment involves several stages. Knowing what happens at each stage can help you plan your questions, visits and expectations.

Step 01

Initial enquiry

Contact the team, explain what you would like to discuss and receive guidance about the next steps and information required.

Step 02

Primary treatment

Following clinical assessment and planning, your first treatment visit may take place in Leipzig, Germany.

Step 03

Healing phase

A healing period is required. The website describes a typical period of around two to six months, depending on the case.

Step 04

Further treatment

A secondary visit is arranged for the next stage of treatment when clinically appropriate, followed by ongoing care guidance.

Planning note: Treatment stages, visit locations and timelines vary according to individual clinical needs. Your treatment team should confirm your personal schedule before you make travel plans.
